DWD ARTWORK IMAGE GUIDE
======================

The Charizard artwork photo is already included as charizard.jpg and is
connected to the homepage hero, featured piece, and mystery gallery. Add the
following owner-approved JPG photographs to this folder as they become
available:

  pikachu.jpg
  gengar.jpg
  custom-familiar.jpg
  fantasy-relic.jpg
  midnight-continue.jpg

The first three names are used by the featured collection and mystery gallery.
The remaining names are used by the custom-art section.

Preparation
-----------
- Use photographs of artwork you own or are authorized to publish.
- Use JPG/JPEG format and preserve the exact lowercase filenames above.
- Crop/compose each photograph around the artwork; square or portrait images
  work well in the responsive card layouts.
- Optimize images for the web. Aim for roughly 300 KB or less per photograph
  where practical; retain enough resolution for clear display.
- Avoid spaces in filenames and do not change the paths in index.html unless
  you also update every corresponding gallery entry.

Connect photos to the page
--------------------------
For each new photo, open index.html, find its artwork container, and add an img
element immediately before that container's fallback span. The Charizard
elements already demonstrate the pattern. For the hero card, use:

  <img src="images/charizard.jpg" alt="" data-artwork-image
       fetchpriority="high" decoding="async">

Use loading="lazy" decoding="async" for the other gallery images. Charizard,
Pikachu, and Gengar appear in both the featured collection and mystery
gallery, so add their img element in both sections. Custom photos belong in
their matching custom-image containers. Keep alt="" when the adjacent button
or caption already provides the artwork name and the image is decorative;
otherwise provide concise descriptive alt text. The existing script detects
successful image loads and preserves the placeholder when no photo is added.

Missing photographs
-------------------
The site includes designed artwork placeholders. Until other JPGs are added
and connected, those panels show their placeholders. If any connected photo
is missing, the placeholder remains visible instead of breaking the layout.

Copyright and identity
----------------------
Use original DWD artwork photography or assets you have permission to display.
Do not add official Pokémon logos, card templates, fonts, or character artwork.

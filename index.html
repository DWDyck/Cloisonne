METAL / GLASS / FIRE

Cloisonné
technique.

Practiced for centuries on jewelry, vessels, and decorative objects, cloisonné is a metal-and-glass art. Fine wire partitions form cells that hold each color.

  1. 01

    Shape the wire

    Fine metal outlines the design, creating separate cells for color.

  2. 02

    Fill and fire

    Glass enamel fills each cell, then kiln firings fuse the color in layers.

  3. 03

    Refine the surface

    Grinding and polishing reveal crisp metal lines and a luminous finish.

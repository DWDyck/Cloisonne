THE ARTISAN BEHIND THE SPECIMENS

A collector first.
A maker now.

I grew up in the golden after-school hours: a handheld glowing in the corner of the room, a stack of cards kept carefully, and the belief that the next discovery could change everything.

Today, I work in metal, fire, pigment, and resin to make original DWD creatures and keepsakes that carry a little of that feeling forward. No mass production. Just patient hands, curious ideas, and objects built to live on your shelf for years.

Every line is placed by hand. Every finish is considered in real light. The small variations are proof a person was here.
